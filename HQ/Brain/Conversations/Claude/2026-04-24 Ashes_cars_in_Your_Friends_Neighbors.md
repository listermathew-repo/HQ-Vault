**You:** What car does Ashe drive in your friends and Neighbors Porsche

**Claude:** In *Your Friends & Neighbors* Season 2, James Marsden's character Owen Ashe is associated with a couple of notable cars. He arrives in a blue McLaren, swerving it directly into the drama of Westmont Village.

As for the Porsche specifically, Ashe drives a Porsche 918 Spyder — which Marsden has called his "dream car." The 918 Spyder can range from under $1 million (MSRP) to many millions of dollars, and the show had a Porsche representative on set keeping a close eye on it at all times.
