**You:** i am based in adeladie
was employed from 31 august 2002 to 6 march 2026

what is my minimum reduncnay pay

**Claude:** ## Minimum Redundancy Pay – Australia (NES)

Based on employment from **31 August 2002 to 6 March 2026**, your total service is approximately **23 years and 6 months**.

Under the **National Employment Standards (Fair Work Act 2009)**, which applies in South Australia, the redundancy pay scale is:

| Years of Service | Weeks' Pay |
|---|---|
| 1–2 years | 4 weeks |
| 2–3 years | 6 weeks |
| 3–4 years | 7 weeks |
| 4–5 years | 8 weeks |
| 5–6 years | 10 weeks |
| 6–7 years | 11 weeks |
| 7–8 years | 13 weeks |
| 8–9 years | 14 weeks |
| 9–10 years | 16 weeks |
| **10+ years** | **12 weeks** |

### Your entitlement: **12 weeks' base pay**

A few important notes:

- The **drop from 16 to 12 weeks** at 10+ years is intentional under the NES — it accounts for the fact that long-service leave entitlements also apply at that tenure.
- Redundancy pay is calculated on your **base rate of pay** for ordinary hours — it excludes overtime, bonuses, penalties, and allowances.
- Your **award, enterprise agreement, or employment contract** may provide a more generous entitlement — the NES is just the legal minimum.
- You may also be entitled to **long service leave** payout (in SA, this accrues after 7 years).
- You should receive payment in lieu of any **unused annual leave** as well.

I'm not a lawyer, so for your specific situation I'd recommend checking with **Fair Work Australia (13 13 94)** or a South Australian employment lawyer to confirm your full entitlements.
