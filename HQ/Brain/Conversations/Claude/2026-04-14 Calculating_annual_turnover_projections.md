**You:** 600 aud turnover per day via a pty and trust

what is the turnover over 200 days

**Claude:** To calculate the turnover over 200 days:

**Daily turnover:** $600 AUD
**Number of days:** 200

**Total turnover = $600 × 200 = $120,000 AUD**

So over 200 days, the turnover would be **$120,000 AUD**.
