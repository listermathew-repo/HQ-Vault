I am writing to formally lodge a complaint regarding the decision to close my Wise account, communicated most recently by "Lilly" on 11 February 2026.

To implement a lifetime ban for a single failed transaction—which Wise’s systems successfully blocked anyway—is a disproportionate response that ignores a six-year history of perfect compliance.
