**Claude:** Here's your cover letter. A few things worth flagging about this application:

**Where your CV is strong:**
- The SAP-to-D365 F&O migration at Capgemini is directly relevant — Business Central and F&O share the same Dynamics 365 family
- The Adaptive Insights CPM and UiPath AR/AP automation at SAGE Group tick the planning/automation boxes nicely
- Your multi-entity, high-growth delivery experience (Toll turnaround, acquisitions context) fits the brief well

**One honest gap to be aware of:**
The role specifically asks for a "strong finance background with deep understanding of accounting processes" — implying someone who has sat closer to the finance function itself (a Finance Manager who then led a system transformation, rather than a Programme Manager who delivered one). The letter frames your experience as strongly as possible, but you may be asked to demonstrate hands-on finance process knowledge in interview. Worth thinking through concrete examples from your Dynamics F&O and Adaptive Insights work where you directly shaped finance process design decisions.
