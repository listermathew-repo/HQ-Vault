**You:** what should I put in here, you know me now...

What __[personal preferences](https://support.anthropic.com/en/articles/10185728-understanding-claude-s-personalization-features)__ should Claude consider in responses?
Your preferences will apply to all conversations, within __[Anthropic’s guidelines](https://www.anthropic.com/legal/aup)__.

**Claude:** I don't actually have any stored memories about your personal preferences yet—each conversation starts fresh for me unless you've set preferences in Claude's settings.

But here's what you could consider adding to make Claude's responses work better for you:

**Communication style:**
- Formal vs. casual tone
- How much detail you typically want (brief summaries vs. thorough explanations)
- Preferred structure (bullet points vs. prose, with/without headers)

**Work context:**
- Your role or industry (helps me calibrate technical depth)
- Tools or platforms you use regularly
- Any jargon or terminology specific to your field

**Content preferences:**
- How you like information organized
- Whether you prefer examples, metaphors, or step-by-step breakdowns
- Any topics you'd rather avoid or focus on

**For this chat specifically:**
If you want me to remember things about you going forward, I can store them using my memory system. For example, you could tell me:
- Your role/profession
- Key projects you're working on
- Communication preferences
- Interests or expertise areas

Then I can use that context naturally in future conversations. Would you like to set any of those up now, or adjust how I'm responding to you?
