- Pty Ltd has higher overheads (~$8k more per year) but gives you more tax planning flexibility — definitely worth chatting to an accountant about which structure suits you better
- These rates don't account for the tax differences between structures, which can be significant with a Pty Ltd
- SA's 12 public holidays (including Adelaide Cup and Proclamation Day) are factored in
