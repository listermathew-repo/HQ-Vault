### **What The Fair Work Act Actually Says**

Under Section 117 of the Fair Work Act, notice period is calculated based on continuous service, plus an additional week if the employee is over 45 years old and has completed at least 2 years of continuous service.

For **4–5 years service:**
- Base notice: 4 weeks. If over 45 with 2+ years service: 5 weeks.
