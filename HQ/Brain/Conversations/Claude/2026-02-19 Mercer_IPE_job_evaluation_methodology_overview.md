**You:** Mercer International Position Evaluation (IPE) System is a globally recognized job evaluation methodology used to assess and grade jobs based on their relative work value.  It evaluates positions using four core factors: Impact, Communication, Innovation, and Knowledge, each broken down into multiple dimensions with defined levels and point scores. 
* Impact assesses the scope and nature of a role’s influence on organizational outcomes, ranging from limited contributions to major strategic influence. 
* Communication evaluates the complexity and scope of interactions, including internal and external communication requirements. 
* Innovation measures the degree of creativity, complexity, and originality required in the role, from routine tasks to scientific or technological breakthroughs. 
* Knowledge considers the breadth and depth of expertise needed, including the application of knowledge across local, intercontinental, or global scales. 
The system uses a point-based scoring model, with a maximum total of 1,225 points.  These points are converted into 48 job levels using a point-to-grade conversion table, which helps standardize job classifications and supports salary benchmarking, compensation management, and career progression planning. 
The Mercer CED Job Evaluation System, a predecessor to IPE, is also used, particularly in public sector organizations.  It is a "points factor" system based on eight sub-factors grouped under three primary dimensions: Expertise, Judgment, and Accountability, and is widely used in government and public employment frameworks. 
Organizations using Mercer’s systems often implement training programs (e.g., online courses from Mercer Learning) to ensure evaluators are certified and consistent in applying the methodology.  Tools like Mercer IPE software support digital job evaluation, data benchmarking, and integration with compensation data for accurate leveling and pay equity analysis.
