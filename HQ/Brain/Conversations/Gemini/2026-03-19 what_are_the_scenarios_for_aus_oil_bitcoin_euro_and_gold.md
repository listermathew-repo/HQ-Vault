**You:** what are the scenarios for aus, oil, bitcoin, euro and gold

**Gemini:** Based on the analysis provided, here are the current trading scenarios for each asset:

  - **Aussie (AUD):** This asset is currently in **scenario four**. While the market has closed above previous highs, there has been no confirmation yet, and rotating is occurring. There is currently no trade for the Aussie.
  - **US Oil:** This asset is being **moved out of the immediate watch list**. Its trading behavior is described as tricky due to large wicks on either side causing liquidity grabs, which makes trending scenarios difficult to follow.
  - **Bitcoin:** This asset is either already in **scenario four** or is about to be, pending a market close. Similar to the Aussie, it had previously shown a scenario two short bias, creating an imbalance beneath the VWOP (Volume Weighted Average Price) before being "hammered" down.
  - **Euro:** This asset is also in **scenario four**. It experienced a significant run-up with closes above previous levels, but it has not yet had a retest or a full-body candle above specific rebound points.
  - **Gold:** This is the only asset currently in **scenario two**. The market bias remains for the short, as it recently broke to the downside and came off a established ceiling level.
