**You:** can you get rid of balck background

**Gemini:** It appears that your spreadsheet is currently in **Dark Mode**, which changes the background to black and the text to white. Since I am still learning and cannot directly change your browser or system settings, I can guide you through the steps to switch back to a light background.**1. Check for Browser Extensions (Desktop)**  
  
Google Sheets on desktop does not have a native "Dark Mode" setting within the menus. If you see a black background, it is likely being caused by a browser extension like "Dark Reader" or "Night Eye."

  - **How to fix:** Look for a dark mode icon in your browser's toolbar (near the address bar). Click it and turn it off or "disable" it for Google Sheets.

**2. Check Google Sheets App Settings (Mobile)**  
  
If you are viewing this on a phone or tablet, the app has its own theme setting:

1.  Open the Google Sheets app.
2.  Tap the **Menu** (three horizontal lines) in the top-left corner.
3.  Tap **Settings**.
4.  Tap **Theme**.
5.  Select **Light**.

**3. Check Cell Formatting**  
  
If only the cells are black but the rest of the Google Sheets interface (like the menus) is white, it might be a specific cell format:

  - **Reset Fill Color:** Select the entire sheet by clicking the empty box in the top-left corner (between "A" and "1"). Then, click the **Fill color** icon (paint bucket) in the toolbar and select **Reset** or **White**.
  - **Clear Formatting:** With the cells selected, go to the **Format** menu and select **Clear formatting**, or use the shortcut **Ctrl + \\** (Windows) or **Command + \\** (Mac).
  - **Conditional Formatting:** Sometimes a rule changes the color automatically. Go to **Format** \> **Conditional formatting** to see if there are any rules that set the background to black.

**4. System Settings**  
  
If your entire computer or browser is in dark mode, Google Sheets may follow that theme.

  - **Windows:** Go to Settings \> Personalization \> Colors and choose "Light."
  - **Mac:** Go to System Settings \> Appearance and choose "Light."
