**You:** what are the 5 scenarions

**Gemini:** The **Market Alignment Framework** defines five authoritative market scenarios used to classify directional permission and control.**The Five Market Scenarios**

| Scenario       | Title                          | Definition                                                                    | Framework Posture                     |
| :------------: | :----------------------------: | :---------------------------------------------------------------------------: | :-----------------------------------: |
| **Scenario 1** | **Buy-Side Control**           | Acceptance is established and held above a key decision zone.                 | **Long permission active**            |
| **Scenario 2** | **Sell-Side Control**          | Acceptance is established and held below a key decision zone.                 | **Short permission active**           |
| **Scenario 3** | **Control Challenged**         | Price is interacting with a decision zone and acceptance is being negotiated. | **Restricted / Observational**        |
| **Scenario 4** | **Control Transfer Attempt**   | Price is attempting to shift control from one side to the other.              | **Observe only**                      |
| **Scenario 5** | **Control Transfer Confirmed** | Acceptance has shifted and is now held in the opposite direction.             | **New directional permission active** |

**Key Principles of the Scenarios**

  - **Classification, Not Instruction**: These scenarios describe market conditions; they are not direct instructions or recommendations to trade.
  - **Permission vs. Execution**: Only these scenarios grant or restrict "permission" to participate in the market. Execution is only evaluated after a scenario (like Scenario 5) is confirmed.
  - **Patience in Uncertainty**: If a scenario is unclear or classified as "Challenged" (Scenario 3), the framework enforces patience and non-participation.
