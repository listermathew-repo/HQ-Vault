**You:** what is the pre entry checklist

**Gemini:** The term "pre-entry checklist" refers to several distinct processes found in your workspace, primarily related to professional trading preparation and workplace safety compliance.**1. Trading: Market Alignment Framework**This checklist is a mandatory "gate" in Module 2 of the Trade Profitably framework. It must be completed before the market open, as the open is reserved for execution rather than analysis.

  - **Core Beliefs**: Internalize that timeframes have fixed roles (Daily for scenario, 1H for structure, 15M for entry) and that levels are "awareness points," not hard barriers.
  - **Key Vocabulary**: Define terms like Daily Open, Previous Day High (PDH), Previous Day Low (PDL), and Weekly Open.
  - **Understanding Check**: Verify the ability to name eight levels and write a four-component bias statement (scenario, direction, entry condition, and invalidation).
  - **Module Completion**: Confirm reading the reference guide, attending sessions, and scoring at least 7/10 on the module quiz.

**2. Workplace Safety: Confined Space Entry**This is a safety tool used by supervisors to identify hazards and determine required precautions before entering a confined space.

  - **Hazard Identification**: Assessment of atmospheric (oxygen deficiency, toxic gases), mechanical, electrical, and physical hazards (heat, noise, engulfment).
  - **Atmospheric Testing**: Initial and continuous monitoring of Oxygen (19.5%–23.5%), LEL (flammables \<10%), Carbon Monoxide (\<35 ppm), and Hydrogen Sulfide (\<10 ppm).
  - **Isolation & Control**: Verification of Lockout/Tagout (LOTO) procedures, ventilation modification, and purging/flushing of the space.
  - **Personnel & Equipment**: Confirmation of trained entrants/attendants, rescue readiness (SCBA, lifelines), and functional communication systems.

**3. IT & Business Operations**

  - **Server Migration**: A pre-migration checklist for Salesforce (SAGE to Action) includes verifying logins via web/API/VPN, testing URL redirects, and checking that all custom tabs, dashboards, and objects function correctly.
  - **Membership Launch**: Guidelines for the "BBGA" pre-launch involve defining a niche, conducting market research, establishing legal structures, and setting membership tiers.
  - **Education (South Australia)**: A "pre-entry" session (one per week) is offered to children in the term before they commence preschool.
