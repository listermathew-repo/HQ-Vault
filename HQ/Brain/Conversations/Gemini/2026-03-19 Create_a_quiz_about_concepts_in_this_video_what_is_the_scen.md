**You:** what is the scenario for euro

**Gemini:** The Euro is currently in **Scenario Four**. This categorization is based on several market observations:

  - **Full Body Close:** The market has closed above a specific level, but there hasn't been a "full body candle" close above it during the most recent rebound.
  - **Lack of Confirmation:** There was a candle that broke the level, but it failed to provide confirmation because there was no close below the low point.
  - **No Retest:** While the Euro saw a massive run-up and went significantly higher, it has not yet come back for a retest.
  - **Tougher to Trade:** Compared to Bitcoin and Gold, the Euro was "tougher to get on" because it lacked the clear "institutional support" and "imbalance" seen in those other assets.
