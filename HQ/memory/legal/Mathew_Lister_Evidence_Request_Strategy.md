## PART 1: THE LEGAL FRAMEWORK FOR EVIDENCE

### Why This Matters

Under Fair Work Act Section 389, a dismissal is only "genuine redundancy" if:

1. ✅ Employer no longer needs job (Capgemini likely satisfies this)
2. ❌ **Employer complied with consultation obligations** ← **NEED EVIDENCE HERE**
3. ❓ **Redeployment was not reasonable** ← **NEED EVIDENCE HERE**

**Burden of proof:** In a Fair Work Commission claim, **Capgemini must prove** they:
- Conducted adequate consultation
- Genuinely considered redeployment
- Found no suitable roles (with documentation)

**Your job:** Force them to produce evidence. If they can't, the claim fails.

## PART 2: HOW TO REQUEST (PROCEDURALLY)

### The Correct Method

**DO NOT:**
- Call and ask verbally
- Email informally ("Can you clarify...?")
- Give them discretion about what to provide

**DO:**
- Send a formal written request (email is fine)
- Be specific about what you want
- Reference the Fair Work Act/redundancy process
- Set a reasonable deadline (7–10 days)
- Keep copies of everything

```

Mathew Lister
```

- Creates a paper trail (email = evidence)

- If assessment was cursory/undocumented = bad faith
- If salary/location obviously unsuitable = employer fettering with prejudices (illegal)

#### **Request 1B: ASSESSMENT NOTES FOR EACH ROLE**

**What to request:**
> "For each role listed in [Request 1A], provide written assessment notes 
> explaining:
> (a) Why the role was deemed unsuitable for my position
> (b) Who made the assessment
> (c) Whether the role requirements exactly matched my current role, or 
>     whether some training/upskilling would have enabled me to perform it
> (d) Whether the role was presented to me as an option
> (e) The date the assessment was completed"

**What proper assessment looks like:**
```
Role: Technical Lead – Cloud Infrastructure, Perth
Date Assessed: 28 February 2026
Assessed By: Anle Chen (HR Business Partner)
Notes: 
  - Requires AWS certification (Mathew does not currently hold)
  - Role based in Perth (Mathew's contract specifies Adelaide)
  - Salary $140k (Mathew's current $150k—potential concern re: pay reduction)
  - Presented to Mathew on 28 Feb; Mathew declined without explanation
  - Alternative: Upskilling available but would take 8 weeks (timeframe too tight)

Assessment: UNSUITABLE due to geographic relocation + cert requirement + timeframe

Assessor signature: _______________ Date: 28 Feb 2026
```

**What bad assessment looks like:**
```
"Technical Lead – Perth – didn't think he'd want it"
"Senior Analyst – doesn't match his background"
"Project Officer – too junior"
(no dates, no names, no genuine consideration)
```

**Why this matters:**
- Fair Work Act requires genuine consideration, not assumptions
- Case law (Khliustova v Isoton) prohibits employers from "fettering offers based on prejudices"
- If roles weren't genuinely offered = breach of consultation
- If upskilling could have enabled role = should have been offered
